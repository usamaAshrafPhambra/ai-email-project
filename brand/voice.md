# Usama Fabric: brand voice

Usama Fabric sells good, long-lasting fabric. Our customers want to hear when new stock arrives, and they want fabric that lasts. We speak like a respected family business: polite, trustworthy and proud of our quality.

## Our voice in three words

1. **Traditional:** we honour craft and the trust built over generations. We don't chase trends or hype.
2. **Decent:** respectful, modest and honest. We never push or pressure.
3. **Joyful:** we share real happiness when new fabric arrives, but calmly. No shouting.

## Words we use

- decent
- premium quality
- trusted by generations
- new arrivals, new stock has arrived
- long-lasting, made to last
- carefully chosen
- thank you for your trust
- Polite forms of address, such as "Dear customer" or "Dear [first name]"

## Words we avoid

- cheap, cheapest, lowest price, bargain
- hurry, last chance, only a few left, don't miss out (unless it's true and we can show it)
- SHOUTING IN CAPITALS, lots of exclamation marks (!!!) and lots of emojis
- slang and trendy words, like "OMG", "insane deal" or "steal"
- Pushy phrasing, like "buy now before it's gone"

## Claims we must never make

No fake claims. Only say what we can prove.

- Never promise that fabric "never fades", "never shrinks", is "100% shrink-proof" or "lasts forever".
- Never call it "the best fabric in the world", "number one" or similar, unless we can show proof.
- Never make up reviews, customer numbers, awards or stock levels.
- Never invent a deadline or a "limited stock" warning that isn't real.
- Never give a fabric's material or origin (for example "pure cotton" or "imported") unless it's confirmed for that fabric.

## How we sign off

Every email ends with:

> Thank you for your trust,
> Team Usama Fabric
