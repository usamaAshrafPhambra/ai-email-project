If you sew at home or take fabric to your own tailor, winter is a lovely time to start something new. Our new winter fabric is here, and we would like to help you choose well.

**A few simple tips:**

- **Think about how it will be worn.** A piece for every day needs fabric that is easy to wash and wear. A piece for a special day can be a little finer.
- **Feel it if you can.** The weight and the fall of a fabric matter as much as its colour. Our close-up photos help, and you can always feel it in the shop.
- **Check the care notes.** Each fabric on our new arrivals page has honest care notes, so you know how to look after it.
- **Ask us how much you need.** Bring your pattern or your tailor's measurements to the shop, or simply reply to this email, and we will gladly help you work out the right amount.

Good fabric is a pleasure to cut and stitch, and it stays with you for a long time. That is why we choose ours with care.
