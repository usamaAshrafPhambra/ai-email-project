# Campaign plan: New Winter Arrivals

Usama Fabric · Email only · Monday 7 December 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027 (4 weeks)

All copy follows `brand/voice.md`. Every email ends with "Thank you for your trust, Team Usama Fabric".

---

## 1. Campaign overview

- **Campaign name:** New Winter Arrivals
- **Summary:** We tell the customers who asked to hear about new stock that our winter fabric has arrived, and we help each group find the right fabric for them.
- **Main goal:** 300 customers place an order from the new winter arrivals between Monday 7 December 2026 and Sunday 3 January 2027.
- **Other goals:**
  - Keep the list healthy: unsubscribe rate stays below 0.5% per email.
  - Learn which customer group responds best, to guide the next new-stock email.

## 2. Audience

About 2,000 people on our customer list. They all asked to hear when new stock arrives. That makes this a "decision" stage audience: they already know and trust us.

| Group | Who they are | What they want | What may hold them back |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Home tailoring and stitching customers** | People who sew at home or take fabric to their own tailor | Good fabric that is easy to cut, stitch and wear for a long time | Not sure how a fabric feels or how much they need |
| **B. Gift and wedding-season buyers** | People buying fabric as a gift, or for weddings and family events this season | Something special that looks and feels premium, ready in time | Worried about timing and choosing for someone else |
| **C. Loyal repeat customers** | People who have bought from us before, some for many years | To see the new stock first, and to be treated with respect | Nothing much, as long as we keep our quality |

Where they spend time: their email inbox, and in our shop. No paid channels in this plan.

## 3. Key messages

**Core message:** Our new winter fabric has arrived, carefully chosen and made to last.

| Supporting message | Best for | Proof we can show |
|---|---|---|
| New stock has arrived for the winter season. | All groups | Real photos of the new fabric on our shelves |
| Carefully chosen fabric that is made to last. | A, C | Close-up photos and our honest care notes for each fabric |
| A thoughtful gift for weddings and family occasions. | B | Photos of fabric gift-wrapped as we really offer it. Only mention delivery times we can keep. |
| Trusted by generations of families. | C | Our real years in business and our family story (confirm the year before use) |

**Call to action:** "See the new arrivals" (link to the shop or new-arrivals page), or "Visit us in the shop".

**Rules for every message:**
- Only state a fabric's material or origin when it is confirmed for that fabric.
- Only mention stock levels or dates when they are real and we can show them.
- No prices in this plan. Add them only once they are confirmed.

## 4. Channel strategy

| Channel | Why it fits | Format | Effort | Budget |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Email** (only channel) | The list asked to hear about new stock. It costs very little and reaches them directly. | Short, polite emails with real photos and one clear link | Low to medium | Our existing email tool. Main cost is our time for photos and writing. |

Not used: paid ads, social ads, influencers. The budget is small and the plan is email only.

## 7. Success metrics

| Metric | Target | How we track it | When we check |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Orders from the new arrivals (main goal)** | **300 by Sun 3 Jan 2027** | Shop orders linked to campaign emails (tracked links or a note at checkout) | Every Monday |
| Open rate | Around 30% or more | Email tool reports | 2 days after each send |
| Click rate | Around 3% or more | Email tool reports | 2 days after each send |
| Orders by group (A, B, C) | Compare groups | Tag each email by group | End of campaign |
| Unsubscribe rate | Below 0.5% per email | Email tool reports | After each send |

The open and click targets are general starting points, not our own past results. Update them once we have our own numbers.

Reporting: a short weekly check every Monday (14, 21 and 28 December), and a final summary on Mon 4 January 2027.

## 9. Risks and how we handle them

| Risk | How we handle it |
|---|---|
| Too many emails tire people, and they unsubscribe | Most emails go to one group only. Nobody gets more than 4 emails in the 4 weeks. Stop the reminders if unsubscribes go above 0.5%. |
| Holiday weeks (late December) mean fewer people read email | Main messages go out early in December. Late-December emails are short and gentle. |
| A popular fabric sells out while emails still show it | Check stock before each send and take sold-out fabric out of the email. |
| Copy makes a claim we can't prove | Run every email through `/marketing:brand-review` against `brand/voice.md` before it goes out. |
